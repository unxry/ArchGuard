# ADR 0016: Hybrid proof precedence and versioned evidence

Status: accepted for PROMPT 009 foundation, 2026-10-05.

Context: deterministic normative conformance, structural observations, semantic assessments and
discovery hypotheses have distinct scientific meanings. Merging them into a weighted score before
ground truth would introduce unsupported confidence and could suppress valid deterministic proof.

Decision: evolve the existing ARCH-only Hybrid boundary with an implemented legacy batch facade
and typed case-level composition. Keep original Finding IDs, separate channels and stable UUIDv5
anchors. Align by IAM identity, directed pairs, real containment and actual graph relations; require
a versioned concern mapping for agreement/conflict. Preserve deterministic Finding/severity as
hard analyzer/result invariants. Default candidate decisions are uncalibrated review or insufficient
evidence with null confidence. Export ordered versioned features, provenance, nullable completeness
and separate feature/evidence fingerprints. Source, raw prompts, credentials and operational IDs
do not enter canonical results. Offline AI artifacts require reconstructed context fingerprint
agreement. The CLI makes zero provider calls.

Consequences: inspectable decisions and future training interchange are available without fake
weights or duplicate Findings. No registry is needed for one executable policy. Weighted/logistic
artifact metadata is defined but fitting, scoring, labels and benchmark claims remain deferred.
Fixed AI input is reproducible; live LLM output is outside that guarantee. Repository-level dataset
splits and calibration will be designed in PROMPT 010 after review.
